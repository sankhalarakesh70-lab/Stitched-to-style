  4. कस्टमाइज्ड ऑर्डर्स रिफंड नहीं होंगे: कपड़ा कटने और सिलने के बाद इसे बदला या वापस नहीं किया जाएगा; कृपया नाप और डिजाइन सावधानी से चुनें।

Terms & Conditions

Important information about orders, custom stitching and payment.

1. Fabric + Stitching Order

Fabric and stitching must be ordered together. Fabric-only and stitching-only orders are not accepted through this website.

2. Order Confirmation

A confirmation amount of ₹99 is required. An order will be considered confirmed only after confirmation by our team on WhatsApp.

3. Payment Method

Payment methods and payment details will be discussed directly with the customer on WhatsApp. No online payment is collected through this website.

4. Fabric Availability

Fabric availability, colour and quantity are subject to stock. Our team will confirm availability before finalising the order.

5. Custom Stitching & Measurements

Customers are responsible for providing correct body measurements and stitching requirements. Please verify all measurements and design details before confirmation.

Once fabric has been cut or stitching work has started, customised orders cannot be returned or refunded. Please confirm your measurements and design carefully before the work begins.

7. Pricing

Prices displayed on the website are estimates. Final pricing, additional customisation charges and delivery charges will be confirmed on WhatsApp.

8. Order Changes & Cancellation

Any change or cancellation request must be discussed with our team on WhatsApp. Changes may not be possible after fabric cutting or stitching has started.

9. Delivery

Delivery availability, charges and estimated delivery timelines will be discussed and confirmed before finalisation.

10. Customer Support

For questions regarding fabrics, stitching, orders or payments, please contact us through WhatsApp or Instagram.
